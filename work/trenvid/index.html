Trenvid

Trending video aggregation platform

A high-performance aggregator that pulls trending videos from YouTube and TikTok in real time, and lets people browse them in one endless, filterable feed.

The problem

Trending lives in too many places.

What's trending on YouTube isn't what's trending on TikTok, and checking both means switching apps and losing the thread. The aim was one place to see what people are watching right now, fast enough that browsing never feels like waiting.

The approach

Pull in real time, load without blocking.

Trenvid fetches trending videos from both platforms in real time and streams them into an infinite-scrolling feed with dynamic filtering. Content loads asynchronously, so the page stays responsive however far someone scrolls.

Engineering highlights

Real-time aggregation

Trending videos pulled live from YouTube and TikTok.

Infinite scrolling

A continuous feed that keeps loading as you browse.

Dynamic filtering

Narrow the feed instantly without reloading the page.

Asynchronous loading

Content is fetched in the background so the interface never blocks.

Custom multi-platform embed system

One embed layer across platforms, with automatic fallbacks when a third-party player fails.

Controlled third-party content

Third-party content is handled deliberately, not dropped straight into the page.
