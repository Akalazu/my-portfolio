The problem

Creator performance is scattered across platforms.

Followers live on one app and engagement on another, with no common yardstick. Brands shortlist creators on hype, agencies stitch together reports by hand, and creators struggle to show their real value. 30 Cycle builds data infrastructure for Africa's creator economy so all three can decide on consistent, comparable signals.

My part

From building the pipelines to leading the delivery.

I started as a backend developer: I architected the PHP + MySQL pipelines that ingest creator metrics from multiple social APIs, and built the RESTful API layer that powers the analytics dashboards. After consistently delivering, I was promoted to Technical Project Manager, and now lead delivery across creator analytics, influence scoring, audience insights and the SME/agency campaign management portal.

What the platform does

Cross-platform aggregation

Creator performance aggregated across key channels and presented through one clear dashboard.

Growth & engagement analytics

Track how an audience grows and how it responds, over time.

Audience insights overview

Understand who a creator reaches, to match partnerships with confidence.

Content performance reporting

See which content works, not just how many people follow.

Influence scoring

A comparable measure that gives brands a common way to evaluate creators.

Campaign management portal

A portal for SMEs and agencies to plan and manage creator campaigns.

Tailored outcomes for each audience

Creators

See which content formats drive growth, track engagement quality rather than volume, and build a performance story for brand deals.

SMEs and brands

Shortlist creators on consistent, comparable signals, reduce wasted spend, and get clean reporting for management approvals.

Agencies

Standardise reporting across creators and platforms, benchmark on one metric framework, and deliver clearer ROI narratives to clients.
