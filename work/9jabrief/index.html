The problem

The news is everywhere, and nobody has time to chase it.

Nigerian readers follow many outlets, each with its own layout, ads and long articles. They want the headlines, from sources they trust, in one place, and they want it to be fast, especially when a big story breaks and everyone arrives at once.

The approach

A brief feed, fast by design.

9jabrief pulls headlines and short excerpts from leading Nigerian outlets and presents them as a clean, scannable feed, with each story crediting its source and linking out to read more. I built it around a real-time pipeline with sub-5-second content latency, layered personalised filtering on top, and treated SEO as a product feature from the start.

Engineering highlights

One feed, many outlets

Headlines from outlets such as Punch, Premium Times, Vanguard, ThisDay, BusinessDay and Nairametrics, each clearly credited.

A feed built for scanning

Headline, source, date, thumbnail and a short excerpt, with Read More for the full story.

Category-first navigation

A swipeable rail: Top News, Sports, Entertainment, Politics, Business, Technology and Stories/Gist.

Sub-5-second latency

Stories reach readers in under five seconds from the source.

Personalised filtering

Improved content discoverability by 40% and boosted retention by 25%.

Built for surges

Sustained 99.9% uptime when traffic spiked.

SEO as a feature

Search improvements drove 50% organic traffic growth over six months.

Accounts

Sign in with Google or email, with refresh and load-more controls to keep the feed current.
