The problem

When a story breaks, everyone arrives at once.

Readers want what happened, right now, and only the parts they care about. That demands near-instant delivery, relevant filtering, and the reliability to hold up when traffic spikes, not just when it's quiet.

The approach

Fast by design, personal by default.

I built the platform around a real-time content pipeline with sub-5-second latency, then layered personalised filtering on top so every reader sees a feed that fits them. SEO was treated as a product feature from the start, not an afterthought.

Engineering highlights

Sub-5-second content latency

Stories reach readers in under five seconds from source.

Personalised filtering

Improved content discoverability by 40% and boosted retention by 25%.

Built for surges

Sustained 99.9% uptime when traffic spiked.

SEO as a feature

Search improvements drove 50% organic traffic growth over six months.

Category-first navigation

A swipeable rail runs from Top News through Sports, Entertainment, Politics, Business and Technology to Stories/Gist.

Always fresh

Live article counts per section and one-tap refresh keep the feed current.
