The problem

Schools run on spreadsheets, notebooks and manual work.

Admissions, attendance, fees, assessments and report cards usually live in separate places, and teachers carry the paperwork. Gagner for Schools replaces that patchwork with a single intelligence layer built around how Nigerian schools actually operate, not adapted from office software.

The approach

One shared intelligence layer, maintained centrally.

I architected the platform as a multi-tenant SaaS: each school gets its own space, while connecting to a shared layer that is maintained centrally. Curriculum understanding, safety rules and platform-wide learning are updated once and reach every school. The principle throughout is human-in-the-loop: the AI drafts, flags and explains, and teachers, parents and school leaders decide.

Engineering highlights

Multi-tenant architecture

Many schools on one platform, each with its own data, all benefiting from a shared, centrally maintained layer.

Curriculum built for Nigeria

Content based on what Nigerian schools teach, from Pre-Nursery through SS3, across multiple exam bodies.

School management

Admissions, attendance, finance, staff and communication in one place.

Parent portal

A single account for parents covering everything that happens in their child's school day.

Computer-based testing

A question bank through to results, with automated result processing.

An always-on safety layer

Every student message and AI reply is checked; serious risk signals stop the conversation and alert adults. Schools cannot switch it off.

PIN-based result access

Results are released through PINs modelled on WAEC's infrastructure.

Audit trail

A record of what was asked, what was returned and which student was involved.

AI for students

A curriculum-linked tutor that reads photos of handwritten problems and answers spoken questions.

Personalised quizzes checked for accuracy, with a leaderboard across the network.

Homework Coach

Answers open questions that no single subject covers, by voice or text.

Financial AI Analyst

Answers questions about fees and spending from a school's live figures.

Special needs support

Built in from the start, not added later.
